Before you ask

What admissions teams usually push back on

Fair questions. Here's the honest answer to each.

"We already have staff answering these questions."

True — and that's the problem, not the counterargument. Your staff aren't bad at answering "what's the fee for BTech CS"; they're just answering it for the hundredth time instead of calling the student who's actually ready to enrol.

What's actually true: AdmiQ doesn't replace your staff — it takes the repeat questions off their plate so their time goes to the students who need a person.

Proof: nothing gets fully automated without oversight — every question AdmiQ isn't confident about lands in a queue your staff review and answer directly.

"AI chatbots make things up. We can't risk that with a prospective student."

That risk is real for chatbots that answer from general internet knowledge — which is exactly why AdmiQ doesn't work that way.

What's actually true: every answer is retrieved from the documents your college actually uploaded — fee sheets, brochures, eligibility criteria. Nothing outside that.

Proof: when a question isn't clearly answered in your documents, AdmiQ says so and hands it to a person instead of guessing.

"Our students call or walk in — they won't use WhatsApp for something this important."

That assumes admissions enquiries are different from every other conversation a student already has on WhatsApp — with friends, family, their school. They aren't.

What's actually true: WhatsApp is already the default channel. Putting admissions there removes a step (no app to download, no form, no waiting for office hours) instead of adding one.

Proof: it's the same app already on their phone — asking a question works exactly like texting a friend.

"We don't have budget allocated for new software this cycle."

Framed as "new software," that objection makes sense. But the real comparison isn't AdmiQ vs. nothing — it's AdmiQ vs. what repeat questions already cost you in staff hours today.

What's actually true: that cost exists whether or not you buy anything. The question is just whether it's visible.

Proof: we'll size it with you — book the 20-minute demo and we'll estimate the actual hours your team spends on repeat questions before you commit to anything.

"How do we know our data stays ours and isn't mixed with other colleges'?"

Fair concern with any multi-customer platform — worth asking, not assuming the answer.

What's actually true: AdmiQ is multi-tenant by design. Each college's documents, conversations, and student data are isolated from every other college on the platform.

Proof: ask us to walk through the isolation model on the demo call — we'll show, not just tell.
