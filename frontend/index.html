The problem

The same questions, over and over

Fees, eligibility, hostels, placements — your staff already know the answers. They just keep giving them, one call at a time.

Repetitive by nature

Every admissions cycle brings the same handful of questions, hundreds of times over.

Scattered across channels

Calls, emails, WhatsApp, walk-ins — with no single place to see what's been asked or answered.

Inconsistent answers

Different staff, different phrasing — sometimes different information entirely.

Time spent on repetition

Hours spent re-answering are hours not spent on students who are actually ready to enrol.

The solution

Your college's information, answering for itself

AdmiQ turns the documents you already have into a WhatsApp assistant that answers in your college's own words — grounded in what you've published, not the open internet. When it isn't sure, it says so, and brings your staff in.

How it works

From your documents to a resolved query

01

Upload what you already have

Fee structures, eligibility criteria, hostel details, placement records — whatever you'd hand a prospective student.

02

Students ask, naturally

On WhatsApp, in their own words — no app to install, no form to fill out first.

03

AdmiQ answers from your documents

Retrieval grounded in what you've actually published — not invented, not guessed.

04

Uncertain questions go to your staff

Anything AdmiQ isn't confident about is queued for a person to review and reply — the student still gets a best-effort answer while they wait.

05

Every conversation builds a profile

Course interest, concerns, guardian involvement, lead score — captured automatically, so staff know exactly who to follow up with, and how.
